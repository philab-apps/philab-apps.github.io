04 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you might wonder.

Does Tongcap record my screen?+

No. Record your screen using iPhone Control Center, then import the video you select into Tongcap. Stitching screen recordings is a Pro feature.

Does it work with every web page?+

Results depend on the website. Expand collapsed content before capturing. Infinite feeds and independently scrolling areas may not be captured in full.

Are my captured images sent to the developer?+

Capture, stitching, and mosaic processing happen on your device. Captured images are not sent to a developer server. See the Privacy Policy for details about other app data processing.
