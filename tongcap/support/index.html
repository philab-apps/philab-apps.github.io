Tongcap is an iPhone app that turns long screens into a single image. Capturing and editing happen on your device, and the images you capture are never sent to the developer.

Contact

philab.help@gmail.com

Please include your device model, your iOS version, and what happened on which screen so we can look into it quickly.

Frequently asked questions

My Pro purchase doesn't show up.
Tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings.

How do I capture a long screen from another app?
Record the screen with "Screen Recording" in Control Center while you scroll, then pick that video in Tongcap.

Where do my captured images go?
They stay on your device, and only go where you choose when you tap Export.
